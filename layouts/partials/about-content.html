About

I've spent 15+ years in infrastructure: first in enterprise consulting across the Automotive, Energy, Telecom and Aerospace sectors, and more recently in SaaS, including regulated Social Care.

Most recently, I ran the cloud migration and cutover of a regulated UK healthcare SaaS platform from private hosting to Microsoft Azure: 12 self-managed MySQL servers consolidated into 5 Azure managed MySQL databases, 850+ tenant schemas, no data loss.

I write here about real infrastructure work: what was done, what broke and what I'd do differently.

Born and raised in France to Pakistani parents, I hold dual British and French citizenship, and live with my Spanish wife and three children. That multicultural, multilingual upbringing helps me connect with people beyond just the technical requirements. Outside of work, you'll usually find me exploring new places and the outdoors, spending time with family, or listening to podcasts.
